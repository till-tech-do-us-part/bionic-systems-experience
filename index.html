Primary Core · Technical Support & IT Operations Autonomous Service Desk

Autonomous IT Troubleshooting, Remediation, and Verified Recovery

Go beyond chatbots that merely summarize knowledge articles or draft replies for technicians. Bionic Systems connects directly into your IT stack to investigate root cause, apply policy-approved remediations, and prove the system is healthy before closing the incident.

  • Intelligent Technical Diagnosis

    Inspects identity logs, endpoint telemetry, application state, and prior incident history to pinpoint root cause instead of bouncing tickets across tiers.

  • Authorized System Remediation

    Executes approved fixes directly—resetting locked credentials, repairing VPN or certificate faults, resolving SaaS entitlements, and restarting failed services.

  • Verified Technical Recovery

    Validates connectivity, permission propagation, and live health telemetry after every change so recurring tickets do not reopen hours later.

  • Structured Specialist Escalation

    When an incident falls outside autonomous policy or requires architectural intervention, escalates to human engineers with complete diagnostic proof.

Autonomous Voice Agents That Resolve Customer Requests

Organizations need the same verified autonomy for non-technical customer interactions. Conversational voice agents understand natural speech, authenticate callers, and complete routine service requests end-to-end without queue hold times.

  • Natural Voice Inquiries & Billing Resolution

    Answers account, order, and invoice questions accurately by querying live CRM and billing records in real time during the call.

  • Autonomous Refunds, Returns & Exchanges

    Checks return eligibility against business policy, initiates RMA shipping labels, issues approved refunds, and confirms transaction completion.

  • Verified Cross-System Outcome & Warm Handoff

    Updates CRM, ERP, and fulfillment systems atomically—and transfers high-touch or exception calls to human agents with full transcript context.
